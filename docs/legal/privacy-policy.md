# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

Vaultime is a local-first desktop application for tracking PC game playtime. This policy explains what data it keeps, where, and why.

## 1. Data on Your Device

Everything below stays on your device unless you choose to use cloud backup:

- **Game library.** Titles, executable paths and install folders.
- **Session history.** Start and end times, runtime, active and idle time, and integrity records.
- **Artwork.** Images found in your game folders or imported by you, plus cached thumbnails.
- **Settings.** Tracking preferences such as the idle threshold.
- **Device identity.** A random ID created on the first start, used to tell your devices apart in your history.
- **Logs.** Technical log files in the app's log folder, used for troubleshooting.

This data lives in the app's data folder on your computer. Vaultime does not send it anywhere on its own.

## 2. Cloud Backup (Optional)

Cloud backup is invite-only and free. If you create an account, the server stores:

- **Account.** Your email address, account role and status, the invite you used, and when the account was created and last signed in.
- **Password.** Only an Argon2id hash, never the password itself.
- **Sign-in sessions.** Hashed refresh tokens with their expiry and the app's user agent.
- **Devices.** A random device ID generated by the app, the device name, the platform and the app version.
- **Backups.** An archive of your local data that is encrypted on your device before upload. The key comes from your backup passphrase, which never leaves your device, so the server cannot read the archive. Next to it the server keeps unencrypted metadata: a label, size, checksum, timestamps, the app version, your device ID and the number of games, sessions and artwork files.

The server does not store IP addresses in its database.

## 3. Data We Do Not Collect

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No crash reports.
- No advertising identifiers or behavioral profiling.
- No data from other programs. To detect games and tell active from idle time, Vaultime reads which programs run, which window is in front and when you last used the keyboard, mouse or a controller. It notices that input happened, never which key or button, and keeps only the resulting active and idle time.

## 4. Third Parties

Cloud backup runs on a server operated by the Vaultime maintainer. No third-party analytics, advertising or cloud storage providers receive your data. App updates are downloaded over HTTPS from the project's release host, which sees your IP address like any web server does.

## 5. Retention

- **Local data** stays until you delete it or the app's data folder.
- **Cloud backups** stay until you delete them in the app. When your account reaches the backup limit, a new upload replaces the oldest backup. Unfinished uploads are removed after about an hour.
- **Accounts** stay until you ask for deletion.

## 6. Deletion

- Delete local data by removing Vaultime's data folder.
- Delete individual cloud backups from the Cloud page in the app.
- To delete your cloud account with all its devices and backups, contact the maintainer.

## 7. Security

- Cloud traffic uses HTTPS.
- Backups are encrypted on your device with ChaCha20-Poly1305 before upload.
- Sign-in secrets on your device are kept in your operating system's credential store.
- Session history is protected against silent edits by hash chains. This detects tampering but cannot prevent it.

## 8. Children

Vaultime is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes are noted in the changelog.
