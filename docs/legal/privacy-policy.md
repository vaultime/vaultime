# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026

Vaultime is a local-first desktop application for tracking PC game playtime. This policy explains what data it keeps, where, and why.

The controller responsible for this data under the GDPR is Dominik Schwimmbeck, reachable at [vaultime@outlook.de](mailto:vaultime@outlook.de).

## 1. Data on Your Device

Everything below stays on your device unless you choose to use cloud backup:

- **Game library.** Titles, executable paths and install folders.
- **Session history.** Start and end times, runtime, active and idle time, and integrity records.
- **What you write down.** The status you give each game, notes on sessions, sessions you add by hand and the reasons you give for corrections.
- **Earlier playtime.** If you import it, the playtime Steam counted for the games in your library and when you last played them there.
- **Artwork.** Images found in your game folders or imported by you, plus cached thumbnails.
- **Settings.** Tracking preferences such as the idle threshold.
- **Device identity.** A random ID created on the first start, used to tell your devices apart in your history.
- **Logs.** Technical log files in the app's log folder, used for troubleshooting.

This data lives in the app's data folder on your computer. Vaultime does not send it anywhere on its own.

## 2. Cloud Backup (Optional)

Cloud backup is invite-only and free. If you create an account, the server stores:

- **Account.** Your email address, account role and status, the invite you used, and when the account was created and last signed in.
- **Password.** Only an Argon2id hash, never the password itself.
- **Sign-in sessions.** Hashed refresh tokens with their expiry and the user agent of the app's built-in browser, which names your operating system and its version.
- **Devices.** A random device ID generated by the app, the device name, the platform and the app version.
- **Backups.** An archive of your local data that is encrypted on your device before upload. The key comes from your backup passphrase, which never leaves your device, so the server cannot read the archive. Next to it the server keeps unencrypted metadata: a label, size, checksum, timestamps, the app version, your device ID and the number of games, sessions and artwork files.
- **Artwork.** Every image is encrypted on your device the same way and uploaded once, then shared by all backups that contain it. The server knows how many images you store, their encrypted sizes and which backups use them. Each image is named by a code made from the image and your key, so the server cannot read an image or match it with anyone else's. Its size stays visible, and covers of well known games have well known sizes, so a size can hint at a game.

The server does not store IP addresses in its database.

This data is processed to provide the cloud backup you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Beta applications.** If you apply for cloud access on the website, the server stores the email address, the platform and the note you enter, and when you applied. They are used only to answer you and to send an invite (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps you ask for before an account exists).

## 3. Data We Do Not Collect

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No crash reports.
- No advertising identifiers or behavioral profiling.
- No cookies and no tracking on the website. It loads nothing from other services, its fonts come from the same server.
- No data from other programs. To detect games and tell active from idle time, Vaultime reads which programs run, which window is in front and when you last used the keyboard, mouse or a controller. It keeps only that input happened, never which key or button, and stores nothing from it but the resulting active and idle time. To find games it reads the install lists of game launchers, and when you import earlier playtime it reads Steam's playtime file once. It keeps only what belongs to games in your library.

## 4. Third Parties

Cloud backup runs on a server operated by the Vaultime maintainer, rented from IONOS SE, which runs the hardware as a processor on the maintainer's behalf. The website, the downloads and app updates come from the same server over HTTPS. Like any web server it sees your IP address while it answers a request, and it keeps no access logs.

To slow down password guessing and form spam, the server counts requests in its memory. Over ten minutes it counts requests to sign in, sign up, renew a sign-in or change a password per IP address, and failed sign-ins per email address and per email address and IP address together. Over an hour it counts beta applications per IP address. It holds addresses and email addresses only as hashes and drops them when their time is up. It never writes these counts, addresses or hashes to disk. This protects the service and its accounts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email to and from [vaultime@outlook.de](mailto:vaultime@outlook.de), such as invites and answers to beta applications, goes through Microsoft Outlook.com. Microsoft processes these messages and the addresses in them to deliver and store them.

No other third parties, such as analytics, advertising or cloud storage providers, receive your data.

## 5. Retention

- **Local data** stays until you delete it or the app's data folder.
- **Cloud backups** stay until you delete them in the app. When your account holds 30 backups, or when its 1 GiB of storage for backups and artwork is full, a new upload replaces the oldest ones once it is stored. Only a backup larger than the whole storage is refused. Unfinished uploads are removed after about an hour.
- **Accounts** stay until you ask for deletion.
- **Beta applications** are deleted once answered, and after 90 days at the latest.
- **Nightly copies.** The server copies its database every night and keeps each copy for up to 16 days. Deleted accounts, backup records and beta applications can stay in these copies for that long.

## 6. Deletion

- Delete local data by removing Vaultime's data folder.
- Delete individual cloud backups from the Cloud page in the app.
- To delete your cloud account with all its devices and backups, email [vaultime@outlook.de](mailto:vaultime@outlook.de).

## 7. Security

- Cloud traffic uses HTTPS.
- Backups are encrypted on your device with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before upload. Each backup carries a check value made from your key, so a wrong passphrase is noticed. It reveals nothing about the key.
- Sign-in secrets and the backup key on your device are kept in your operating system's credential store.
- Session history is kept in hash chains, so silent edits can be detected. They cannot be prevented.

## 8. Children

Vaultime is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Your Rights

The data on your device is yours to read at any time. Settings, Export saves your sessions as CSV or JSON, and a local backup copies all of it.

You can ask for a copy of the data the cloud server holds about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to its processing. Write to [vaultime@outlook.de](mailto:vaultime@outlook.de). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
