# How to see your playtime in the Xbox app

The Xbox app on Windows does not put your playtime next to your games. Xbox keeps the minutes you played in each game's stats on your Xbox profile, for games that report them.

## On your Xbox profile

1. Sign in at account.xbox.com and open your profile from your gamerpic.
2. Open **Achievements** and pick a game.
3. Open its achievements. The game's stats show the time you played it.

## What Xbox counts

- Games that support Xbox, on console and on PC.
- Not every game reports the stat, so some games show no time at all.
- Long totals have been shown in days rather than hours.

Xbox summed up the years 2023 and 2024 in a Year in Review, and none came out for 2025.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that finds the games of the Xbox app and PC Game Pass on your PC and counts every session, with active time and idle time apart.

- A total for every game, also the ones that report no stat to Xbox.
- A journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- Your Xbox games in one library with the games of every other launcher.

Vaultime counts from the day you install it. Only Steam's earlier playtime can be read in.
