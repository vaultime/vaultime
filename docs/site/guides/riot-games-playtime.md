# How to see your playtime in League of Legends and VALORANT

Riot shows no lifetime playtime anywhere. League of Legends shows your time this season, and the Riot Client and VALORANT show none.

## League of Legends

1. Open your **Profile** in the League client and go to **Stats**.
2. Your time played this season is shown beside your games played. The season menu switches to earlier seasons.
3. The stats of each champion include your time played with them.

## VALORANT and the Riot Client

Neither shows how long you played. Your personal data from Riot, which you can request from Riot Support, holds no playtime either.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that counts every session on your PC, with active time and idle time apart. On Windows it finds your Riot games, on Linux the ones you installed through Lutris.

- One total for each game that never resets with a season.
- A journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- Your Riot games in one library with the games of every other launcher.

Vaultime counts from the day you install it. Only Steam's earlier playtime can be read in.

See also [How to see all your playtime in one place](all-your-playtime.md).

This page was checked in November 2026. Launchers change their apps, so some labels may differ.
