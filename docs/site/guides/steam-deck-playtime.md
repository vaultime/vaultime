# How to see your playtime on the Steam Deck

The Steam Deck counts your play into the same Steam account as your PC, so a game's playtime on the Deck is your total from every device you play it on.

![A player on a couch at night with a handheld gaming PC and a cat asleep beside them](../art/handheld.jpg "Time on the Deck counts into your Steam account.")

## On the Deck

1. Open your **Library** and pick a game. Its page shows when you last played it and your playtime.
2. To list your games by time, sort the library by **Hours Played**.

The number is your account total, not the Deck's share. Steam Replay at the end of the year splits your hours between Windows, Linux and the Deck.

## What it leaves out

- Games you add as non-Steam shortcuts do not count toward your Steam profile or your year in Steam Replay.
- Like everywhere in Steam, the total has no history of your days.

## Bringing Deck play into Vaultime

[Vaultime](/) counts the games on your Windows or Linux PC. For play on the Deck, open the game's page in Vaultime and pick **Add a session**. Give the start, the length and where you played, and for a Steam game mark it as **Played through Steam**. The session counts as active time and is labeled Manual, and it is never counted a second time when you import your Steam totals. See [The journal and stats](../docs/journal-and-stats.md).

See also [How to see your playtime on Steam](steam-playtime.md).

This page was checked in November 2026. Launchers change their apps, so some labels may differ.
