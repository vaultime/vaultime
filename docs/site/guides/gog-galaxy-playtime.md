# How to see your playtime in GOG Galaxy

GOG Galaxy shows your game time on each game's page, also for games of other platforms you connected to it. It adds up what it tracked and what those platforms report.

## In GOG Galaxy

1. Open a game in your library. Its page shows your total game time.
2. In the list view you can add a column with the game time and sort by it.

## What GOG Galaxy counts

- Games you start or Galaxy detects, from GOG and from connected platforms.
- Games you added by hand and linked to their program.
- Hours that connected platforms report, where an integration does that.

A game you own on two platforms gets both totals added together, which can count the same play twice. Players also report that a game started from a desktop shortcut is not tracked.

GOG also sums up your past year on gog.com as Your year with GOG. The edition for 2025 came out in January 2026.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that counts the game that runs, however you started it, a desktop shortcut included.

- Link a game you own on GOG and on Steam, and it counts as one game while each launcher keeps the time played through its copy. Nothing counts twice.
- Active time and idle time apart, and a journal of your days.
- GOG games you play through [Heroic](linux-playtime.md) on Linux count too.

See also [How to see all your playtime in one place](all-your-playtime.md).
