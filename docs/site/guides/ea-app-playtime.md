# How to see your playtime in the EA app

The EA app shows a total for each game on the game's page in your library, with the day you last played it.

## In the EA app

1. Open your **Library** and pick a game.
2. The game's page shows when you last played it and your time played.

The EA app also has playtime controls in its settings, where you can look at your playtime and set limits.

## What the EA app counts

- One total per game, with no history of your days and no split between playing and idling.
- Players have long reported totals that stop updating or drop back after an update, and hours from Origin that never came over.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that finds your EA games and counts every session on your PC, with active time and idle time apart.

- Each session stays in the record, so a total never drops back on its own.
- A journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- Your EA games in one library with the games of every other launcher.

Vaultime counts from the day you install it. Only Steam's earlier playtime can be read in.

See also [How to see all your playtime in one place](all-your-playtime.md).
