# How to see your playtime on Battle.net

The Battle.net app shows no playtime for any game. Some Blizzard games show it inside the game, each in its own way.

## Inside the games

- **World of Warcraft**: type `/played` in the chat. It prints the total time played and the time at the current level, for the character you are playing only. There is no total for your account.
- **Overwatch 2**: your Career Profile shows your time played for each mode and hero.
- **Diablo IV**: players report no playtime readout on PC.

The game time on your Blizzard account pages is the subscription time of World of Warcraft, not time you played.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that counts every session, with active time and idle time apart. On Windows it finds the games Battle.net installed, on Linux the ones you installed through Lutris.

- A total for every Blizzard game, in hours and minutes, across all your characters.
- A journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- Your Blizzard games in one library with the games of every other launcher.

Vaultime counts from the day you install it. Only Steam's earlier playtime can be read in.

See also [How to see all your playtime in one place](all-your-playtime.md).

This page was checked in November 2026. Launchers change their apps, so some labels may differ.
