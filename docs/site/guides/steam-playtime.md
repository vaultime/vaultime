# How to see your playtime on Steam

Steam shows how long you played each game on the game's page in your library and on your profile. It keeps a total for each game, the last two weeks and the day you last played, but no history of your days.

## In the Steam app

1. Open **Library** and pick a game.
2. The bar beside the Play button shows **Last Played** and **Play Time**, your total for that game.
3. To compare games, sort a collection or the All Games shelf by **Hours Played**.

## On your profile

Your profile, in the Steam app or on steamcommunity.com, lists your recent activity with the hours of the past two weeks and the hours on record for each game. The Games page of your profile lists the totals of all your games.

Edit Profile, Privacy Settings, Game details decides who sees them. **Always keep my total playtime private** hides your totals even when others can see your games, and **Mark as Private** on a single game hides that game.

## What Steam counts

- The time a game you started through Steam runs, also while you are away from it. Steam does not tell playing from idling.
- Games you add to Steam as shortcuts do not count toward your profile.
- Players report that time played in offline mode does not always reach the total.

## Steam Replay

Each December, Steam Replay sums up your year on Steam: the games you played, your top games as a share of your playtime, your months, your longest streak and your achievements. It covers 1 January to the middle of December, comes out a few days later and leaves out play in offline mode. Open it from the banner on the store front page.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that counts every game you start, Steam games included, and keeps active time apart from idle time.

- It reads your Steam totals under Settings, Earlier playtime, **Import from Steam**, and counts on from there without counting anything twice.
- Every session lands in a journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- **Year in games** counts your whole year, offline play and every other launcher included, whenever you open it.
