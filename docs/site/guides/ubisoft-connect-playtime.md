# How to see your playtime in Ubisoft Connect

Ubisoft Connect shows your time played in a game's statistics, for games that send them. You find them in the PC app, in the overlay during play, in the mobile app and on the Ubisoft Connect website.

## In Ubisoft Connect

1. Open a game in Ubisoft Connect.
2. Its statistics show your time played, when the game has a statistics page.

## What Ubisoft Connect counts

- The time each game reports in its own statistics, so some games show none.
- Ubisoft says an update can take 24 to 48 hours, statistics sometimes stop updating, and nobody can reset them.

## What Vaultime adds

[Vaultime](/) is a free app for Windows and Linux that finds your Ubisoft games and counts every session as you play, with active time and idle time apart.

- A total for every game, also the ones without statistics.
- A journal of your days, with when you played and for how long.
- Your Ubisoft games in one library with the games of every other launcher.

Vaultime counts from the day you install it. Only Steam's earlier playtime can be read in.
