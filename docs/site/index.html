How it counts

Three kinds of time, kept apart.

Runtime

How long the game was open.

Active time

The game was in front and you were at the keyboard, mouse or controller. This is your playtime.

Idle time

The game ran while you were away or it sat in the background. You choose after how many minutes that starts.

Sleep

Time while your computer sleeps is never counted.

Trust labels

Every session keeps a hash-linked log. A session is Suspicious when the clock jumped and Recovered when Vaultime rebuilt it after an unclean exit, and it says what happened. The labels show changes, they cannot prove that nothing changed.

What you get

A library that looks like one, not a log file.

Your library in its own colors

Opens on the game you play now or last, tinted from its cover, with the week in numbers and every game below.

A journal of your play

Week by week, a 24 hour strip for each day and every session written as a sentence.

Always in view

A live bar shows the running game and its timer, and Ctrl+K jumps to any game or page.

Finds your games

Steam, Epic, GOG Galaxy, Heroic and Lutris, the folders of other launchers on every drive, or any game by hand.

Runs in the background

Closing the window keeps tracking in the tray, and Vaultime can start when you log in.

Backups

Local backups to any folder. Optional cloud backup for invited accounts, encrypted on your PC before upload, free and without tiers.
