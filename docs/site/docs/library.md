# Home and the library

Home shows what you play now, All games holds every game, and the rail on the left sorts your library by launcher.

## Home

Home leads with the game you play now, or the one you played last, in the colors of its cover, and says how far its next [hour mark](game-pages.md#totals) is once it gets close. Below it are your last seven days in numbers, with the time played, the share of it that was active, the sessions and the longest one, and a shelf of the games you played lately, which ends with a link to all your games.

With no games yet, Home says **Start your library** and offers **Discover games** and **Add a game**.

## The rail

The rail on the left lists Home, the journal and the stats, then your library:

- **All games**, with how many games you have.
- **Favorites**, the games you marked with a heart.
- A row for each launcher with its number of games, the launchers with the most games first. Games found in a folder and games you added by hand come last, under **Found in folders** and **Added by hand**.
- **Recently played**, the five games you played last, with the one running now marked **Playing now**.

The **+** next to Library opens **Discover games**. Settings sits at the foot of the rail, with Cloud while cloud backup is on, beside the game Vaultime is tracking.

## Launchers

A launcher's row opens its games: the ones with a copy from that launcher. Above them a sentence says how long you played through it and names the game with most of that time, for example *Eight hundred ten hours through Steam, most of it in Elden Ring.*

- A game you own in two launchers, and linked under **Counts as**, shows under both. Each launcher counts the time its own copy recorded, so the two parts add up to the game's time. See [Game pages](game-pages.md#one-game-two-installs).
- A game played on another PC counts for the launcher of the copy there.
- Playtime imported from Steam counts on the Steam page, and its sentence says it apart: *Steam counted ninety hours before Vaultime.*

The command palette finds launchers too. Type a launcher's name after ++Ctrl+K++.

## Favorites

The heart beside the status on a game's page makes it a favorite, and so do **Add to favorites** in the menu of its cover and the command palette on its page. A favorite shows a small heart under its cover, and the **Favorites** row in the rail opens just those games. Favorites travel with your backups.

## All games

All games shows every game as its cover, with its time and when you last played it. A badge marks a game with a Suspicious or Recovered session in the last 30 days.

Find a game
: The field at the top filters by title, the titles of linked installs included. ++Ctrl+F++ jumps there from anywhere.

Status
: **All**, **Backlog**, **Playing**, **Finished** and **Dropped** show the games with that status, each with its count. Statuses no game has stay out.

Group
: **Launcher**, **Last played**, **Status** and **PC** split the games under headings. Last played reads **Past week**, **Past month**, **Past year**, **Longer ago** and **Not played yet**. A game with copies from two launchers or on two PCs shows in both groups. **PC** is there once the sessions of another PC are merged.

Sort
: **Last played**, **Recently added**, **A to Z** or **Most played**. On a launcher's page, Most played goes by the time on that launcher.

Vaultime remembers the sort and the groups.
